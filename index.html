Your screen stays yours

Screen sharing is off at the start of every call. What you share goes only to your Hermes, never to the voice service, and Speakeasy never captures password managers or a password you're typing.

Speakeasy has no servers. Your voice goes to the voice service on your own account; everything else stays with your Hermes. Privacy

Questions.

What does it cost?

Speakeasy is free and open source. Calls run on your ChatGPT or SuperGrok plan, or on an OpenAI API key billed per minute.

Which AI does the actual work?

Your Hermes, with whatever model you've given it. The voice only talks to you and hands things off.

Where does Hermes need to run?

Wherever you already run it: the same Mac, a Mac mini at home, a Linux box. Tailscale connects them privately, with nothing opened to the internet.

What leaves my computer?

Your voice goes to the voice service you picked, on your account. Jobs and results stay with your Hermes.

What does the voice know about me?

A short summary Hermes writes about you and what it can do for you. You can read and edit it in Settings.

How do I remove it?

Ask Hermes to remove Speakeasy, then delete the app.
