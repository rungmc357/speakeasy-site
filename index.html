Details.

#

Channels

Give your chats a topic, like work or family, and tasks go to the right one. Or say “put this in work.”

↺

Follow-ups

Ask about a task that's already running and it continues that task instead of starting a new one.

✓

Approvals

The voice can't approve anything. Emails, purchases and anything else Hermes checks with you still wait for you.

⌁

Cost

GPT-Live-1 runs on your ChatGPT plan. Or use an OpenAI API key, billed per minute.

⇆

Hermes on another computer

Hermes on a Mac mini at home, you on a laptop somewhere else. Speakeasy connects them over Tailscale, not the open internet.

⌘

The app

Lives in your menu bar. One shortcut starts a call, and a small panel shows what Hermes is working on.

Setup.

Send this to your Hermes, in whatever chat you use with it:

Set up Speakeasy for me: https://speakeasyvoice.ai/setup.md

  1. Hermes installs Speakeasy on the computer it runs on.
  2. Restart Hermes when it asks.
  3. Open the link it sends you on your Mac. It installs the app if you don't have it, then connects.
Set it up yourself 
# on the computer that runs Hermes
hermes plugins install rungmc357/speakeasy#plugin/speakeasy --enable
hermes voice setup

FAQ

What leaves my computer?

Your voice goes to OpenAI, on your account, so GPT-Live-1 can hear you. Tasks and results stay with your Hermes. Speakeasy has no servers.

What does it cost?

Speakeasy is free. The voice runs on your ChatGPT plan, or on an OpenAI API key billed per minute.

How do I remove it?

Ask Hermes to remove Speakeasy, or turn the plugin off yourself, then drag the app to the Trash.

What does the voice know about me?

A short summary Hermes writes: who you are, what you're working on, what it can do for you. You can read and edit it in the app's settings.
